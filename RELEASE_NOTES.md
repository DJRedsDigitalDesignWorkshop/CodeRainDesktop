CodeRainDesktop 1.0.0 brings the CodeRainSaver 1.1.21 visual template to animated macOS desktop wallpaper.

- Native cached glyph and column rendering, with a default 30 fps efficient mode and optional 60 fps.
- Menu bar controls for appearance, pause/resume, and launch at login.
- Animation pauses during sleep and screen lock; desktop icons stay clickable.
- Independent settings; quitting reveals your previous wallpaper.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 13 or later. Open the DMG, drag CodeRainDesktop to Applications, then launch it.

The DMG is Developer ID signed, Apple notarized, and stapled. Author: djred.
